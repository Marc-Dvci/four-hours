# Positioning review

Reviewed 1 November 2026. DORA reporting in Jira is an existing product category.
The [ClearLane Marketplace listing](https://marketplace.atlassian.com/apps/1866864788/clearlane-breach-incident-reporting-nis2-gdpr-dora)
advertises DORA, GDPR and NIS2 reporting, qualification, prepared notifications,
submission receipts, evidence exports and read-only Rovo. This is a review of the
vendor's public claims, not an independently tested feature comparison.

Four Hours demonstrates a narrower evidence-led workflow. Its public source
exposes the classification rules and boundary cases; preserves unknown values;
keeps a first major deadline across later attestations; creates a separate
collective record with original incident snapshots; and exports a full evidence
bundle that a separate verifier can compare with an externally retained digest.
The demo makes these properties inspectable. It does not claim that competing
products lack them.

The useful evaluation is whether an officer can review the decision and challenge
its evidence. Synthetic rule conformance is measured. User time saving, operational
effectiveness, authority acceptance and production platform reliability require
separate validation. A complete multi-regulation portal is outside this prototype's
scope.
