# Terms

Four Hours source code is licensed under MIT. The app supports evidence review, classification attestations and reporting preparation. An authorised officer reviews the incident facts, legal interpretation, applicable authority form and actual submission.

An application attestation records an authenticated account, timestamp and evidence digest. Organisations determine its use in their internal control process. Reports are submitted through the competent authority's own channel and then recorded with a receipt in Four Hours.

Atlassian's platform, usage, Rovo and connector terms apply to the installation. The synthetic replay uses fictional data for product evaluation.
