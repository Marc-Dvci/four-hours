# Four Hours: incident decisions with a reporting clock

Track: Apps for Specialized Industries (finance). Lead bonus: Teamwork Graph. Additional bonuses: Agents, MCP.

An ICT risk officer at an EU bank opens a payments incident in Jira Service Management, checks sourced thresholds, signs a classification, and prepares the notification before its deadline.

This needs Jira Service Management because the incident, its evidence and its accountable owner already live there. The Teamwork Graph adapter indexes synthetic technical services and reads explicit mappings back into sourced evidence. Recurrence searches readable incidents in Forge storage. The Confluence adapter publishes a review workpaper. Live platform verification is tracked in the release checklist.

## Judge path

1. Open the incident desk. The credential-free replay is labelled synthetic.
2. Read the seven criteria, with source, observed time, values and legal references.
3. Change an evidence value; see the deterministic assessment change.
4. Sign the major classification. A signature binds the evidence hash and authenticated identity.
5. Prepare an initial report; record the actual submission time and authority reference.
6. The intermediate clock starts from submission. The final clock follows the latest intermediate report.
7. Find recurring incidents, inspect service dependencies, verify the hash chain and export the workpaper.

## Rule boundary

Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1772 Articles 6, 8 and 9; reporting: Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/301 Article 5; template mapping: Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/302. GDPR Article 33 runs separately from personal-data-breach awareness. The code makes decisions. Rovo proposes evidence-cited narrative and has read-only actions.

Unknown evidence propagates as INCOMPLETE whenever it can alter the result. The critical-services gate is separate from the six materiality groups. Multiple client or transaction sub-thresholds count as one group. Data loss under Article 9(5)(a) counts as one group; only Article 9(5)(b) provides the special route in Article 8(1)(a).

Strictly greater thresholds stay strict: 10%, 100,000 clients, 30%, 24 hours, 2 hours, EUR 100,000. Geography is two or more distinct EU Member States. Missing and invalid denominators cannot produce a zero percentage.

## Architecture

One TypeScript domain engine serves a React Custom UI and Forge resolvers. Local replay uses a per-browser store. Forge uses authenticated Jira reads, application storage, Teamwork Graph and Confluence. Live and replay modes are visible. Signed classifications and report receipts append revisions with SHA-256 hashes. Stale evidence requires a new signed assessment. No action sends a report to a regulator.

## Visual identity

Confirmed by Marc on 1 November 2026: air-traffic-control desk and Swiss railway timetable. Ink navy, white and signal orange; large clocks, flat evidence rows, no card grid or cream grounds. System fonts, visible focus, keyboard controls, colour-independent status labels and reduced-motion support.

## Evaluation and audit

Labelled classification cases are frozen before implementation. Test boundary operators, incomplete inputs, recurring-incident overlap, late classification, calendar months, DST, separate GDPR awareness, stale signatures, forged identities, prompt injection, evidence tampering and report sequencing. Browser checks exercise the actual product and axe-core. Only measured results enter public claims.
